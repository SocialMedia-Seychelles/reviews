A product made for Reels

Your look is already lovely: the turtle-shell logo, soft highlight covers and that turquoise everywhere. The opportunity is more video with stronger hooks, and posts that give people a reason to comment, share and book.

What's working

  • A one-of-a-kind product. "Seychelles' first clear boat" is a scroll-stopper by itself. Nothing else on people's feeds looks like it.
  • Real guests having a great time. Families, couples and kids lying on the deck looking down make the best content.
  • Proof of quality. The Service Excellence Award 2026 and your Reviews highlight build trust before people book.
  • Lots to sell. Daily and private tours, sunset cruises, proposals, and add-ons like charcuterie, prosecco and transfers.
  • A good habit. Around two posts a week since September, so the account already feels active.

What's holding it back

  • Reels aren't reaching new people yet. The latest two got 13–15 likes. Sharper first-second hooks and Reels every week are how new visitors find you.
  • Few reasons to comment. Most posts get 0 comments. Your giveaway got 114, which shows your audience will talk when they're asked.
  • The same angle comes up a lot. Many shots look forward from the bow. Mixing in the view down through the hull, close-ups, the crew and guest reactions keeps the grid fresh.
  • Three platforms, one style. TikTok and Facebook each work a little differently, with their own text styles, sounds and formats, so the same file doesn't always land the same way.
