170 years of stories, in one house 🏛️ Built in 1855 as Henley Villa, it was renamed Kenwyn House by the Eastern Telegraph Company. It was declared a national monument in 1984 and restored by Gran Kaz in 2018. Today it is the Kenwyn House Gallery: a public space for art, culture and community. Shama walks us through its story 🎥 Artists exhibit here for free and keep full control of their sales, and entry is free for everyone. Do you remember Kenwyn House before it was a gallery? Share your memory in the comments 👇 📍 Kenwyn House, Victoria · Mon–Fri 9am–5pm (closed 1–2pm for lunch) · Sat 9am–1pm #KenwynHouse #SeychellesHistory #Victoria #NationalMonument #SeychellesArt
