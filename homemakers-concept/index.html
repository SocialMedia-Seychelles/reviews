    Years of Seychellois homes

    P&J Supplies started as a building materials supplier and grew, one department at a time, into Homemakers: the place you can buy the cement for your walls, the tiles for your floor and the sofa for your living room.

    We still work the way we started. Real people on the shop floor, advice when you need it and honest prices on quality brands like Luminarc, Ronseal, Indesit and Alcolin.
